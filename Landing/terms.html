Visitor information

Terms & Conditions

These guidelines describe how to use the UniServe visitor portal for the Office of Student Services (OSS), PUP Santa Maria Campus. Review them before submitting a walk-in visit or appointment request.

Version 2026-11-07. These terms concern use of this portal. They do not replace University regulations or the requirements of the office handling your request.

Acceptance and your choice

Select Accept to acknowledge these portal rules before submitting a walk-in visit or appointment request. Select Decline to leave the request unsubmitted; you may contact OSS to ask about other ways to obtain assistance. Accepting these terms does not waive your legal rights or provide blanket consent for unrelated processing of personal information.

1. Using the portal

Use UniServe to request OSS services, join the walk-in queue, request appointments, and follow your visit. Provide accurate information and upload only documents relevant to your request. Do not impersonate another person or submit misleading requests.

2. Walk-in visits

Walk-in registration is available during office hours. After joining the queue, stay near OSS and monitor your visit status. Bring the documents listed for your selected service. Queue progress depends on staff availability, service needs, and applicable priority arrangements.

3. Appointment requests

Submitting a preferred date and time creates a request. Your appointment remains pending until OSS confirms it. Staff may approve or reschedule your request. Check your visit status and email for updates before travelling to campus.

4. Arrival and service requirements

For a confirmed appointment, arrive on the confirmed date and show your Visit Pass to OSS staff so they can record your arrival. Late arrivals and missed appointments are handled by OSS. Submitting a request or receiving a queue number does not confirm that all requirements for the requested service have been met.

5. Keep your visit information secure

Save your Visit ID and keep verification codes and private records secure. Use only your own visit details to access records. If you lose your Visit ID or notice incorrect information, contact OSS for assistance.

6. Respectful use

Use messages and feedback respectfully. Do not send threats, harassment, spam, or unrelated documents, and do not attempt to access another visitor's records or disrupt the portal.

7. Personal information

The portal asks for identifying and contact details, service and visit information, and documents relevant to your request. It also handles verification, visit updates, messages, feedback, and activity records. Read the data privacy notice before submitting. Avoid including unrelated personal information, particularly information about other people.

Republic Act No. 10173 and its implementing rules require lawful processing, transparency, a legitimate purpose, and proportionality. Accepting portal rules alone does not establish a lawful basis for every use of personal data. Where separate consent is required, it must be specific and informed.

Subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions, your privacy rights include being informed, access, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, portability, damages, and filing a complaint. You can raise a concern with OSS or use the privacy contact provided in the PUP Privacy Statement. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission. Acceptance of these terms does not remove these rights.

8. Availability and assistance

Portal access may be interrupted by maintenance or technical issues. If you cannot submit or track a request, contact OSS for assistance. Refer to the portal's current service requirements and OSS instructions for your visit.

9. Requests, records, and corrections

Review your information before confirming. A submission records a request; it is not a guarantee of approval, a completed service, or a decision on a student case. If information or a visit status appears wrong, ask OSS to check it. Do not submit another person's request unless you are authorized and OSS permits that process.

10. Applicable rules and changes

Use of the portal remains subject to applicable Philippine law and University rules. These terms do not create additional disciplinary penalties, waive University duties, or limit remedies provided by law. Check the version shown above when reviewing the terms. Questions about the applicable service process should be directed to OSS.

Legal and policy references

For further information, refer to the PUP Privacy Statement, the Data Privacy Act implementing rules (Sections 17–22 and 34–39), and the NPC guidance on data subject rights.
