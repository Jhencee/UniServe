Frequently asked questions

What should I bring?
Each service lists its own requirements in the section above. If you schedule ahead, you can upload the documents when you submit your request. If you walk in, bring the physical documents to the counter.
What happens if I'm late for my appointment?
You have a grace period of 15 minutes after your scheduled time. After that you're marked as late, but you can still check in and OSS staff will review your arrival. If you arrive more than 60 minutes late, the appointment is marked as a no-show. Staff can still review and correct it, so speak to the front desk.
Who gets served first?
Senior citizens are served first (priority lane under RA 9994). After that, visitors who checked in for a scheduled appointment are served before walk-ins, once their scheduled time arrives. Within each group, it's first come, first served.
How do I check my visit, or find my Visit ID?
Open Check My Visit and enter your Visit ID to see your live status. Your Visit ID is printed on your Visit Pass and in your confirmation email. If you've lost it, ask the front desk.
Can I use the portal outside office hours?
No. The visitor portal, including appointment requests, check-in, visit tracking and chat, is open only during office hours (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM). It closes automatically after hours and reopens as soon as the office opens.
