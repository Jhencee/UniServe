Questions

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring?
Each service lists its own requirements in the section above. For an appointment request, you can upload supporting documents when you submit your details. If you walk in, bring the physical documents to the counter.
What happens if I'm late for my appointment?
You have a grace period of 15 minutes after your confirmed time. After that, your arrival may be marked late. Show your Visit Pass to OSS staff so they can record and review your arrival. If no arrival is recorded after 60 minutes, your appointment may be marked as a no-show. Speak to OSS staff if you still need assistance.
Who gets served first?
Senior citizens and persons with disabilities receive priority service. After that, visitors whose arrival has been recorded by OSS for a confirmed appointment are served before walk-ins, once their scheduled time arrives. Within each group, it's first come, first served.
How do I check my visit or recover my reference?
Open Check My Visit using the reference on your Visit Pass. A pending appointment has a request reference; a Visit ID is assigned when the schedule is confirmed. To find previous visits, open History in the visitor portal and verify the email used for your visit. Visitors without email can ask OSS staff for help at the counter.
Can I use the portal outside office hours?
Yes. Appointment requests and Check My Visit remain available when the office is closed. Walk-in queue entry and messages to OSS are available during office hours, following the office calendar, lunch breaks, holidays and special closures. OSS reviews appointment requests during office hours.
Why do I need to verify my email?
Verification confirms that you can access the inbox used for your visit. Enter the code sent to that email to view your visit history or perform protected actions. History reuses your verified session until it expires or you choose a different email.
Why is walk-in intake closed while the office is open?
Intake may stop before closing when the queue reaches capacity or staff set an earlier cutoff. Visitors already in the queue can still be served during office hours. Check the office calendar for lunch breaks, holidays and special hours, or request an appointment.
